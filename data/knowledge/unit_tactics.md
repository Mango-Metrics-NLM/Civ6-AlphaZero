# Unit Tactics Guide

## Unit Classes

### Melee Units
- **Warriors/Swordsmen**: Front-line fighters, benefit from flanking
- **Pikemen/AT Crews**: Anti-cavalry specialists
- **Man-at-Arms/Infantry**: High-strength general purpose

**Tactical Use**:
- Position on defensive terrain when holding
- Use for flanking bonuses when attacking
- Keep adjacent to ranged units for protection

### Ranged Units
- **Archers/Crossbowmen**: 2-tile range, vulnerable to melee
- **Field Cannons/Artillery**: 2-3 tile range, bonus vs cities

**Tactical Use**:
- Stay behind melee screen
- Focus fire on damaged enemies
- Target high-value units first

### Cavalry
- **Horsemen/Knights**: High mobility, weak to anti-cavalry
- **Light Cavalry**: Reconnaissance and pillaging

**Tactical Use**:
- Flank around enemy lines
- Pillage improvements to deny resources
- Chase down retreating units

### Siege Units
- **Catapults/Bombards**: Essential for city attacks
- **Battering Rams/Towers**: Support units for city assault

**Tactical Use**:
- Protect with melee units
- Focus on city walls first
- Multiple siege units speed conquest

### Naval Units
- **Galleys/Quadriremes**: Coastal control
- **Frigates/Battleships**: Long-range naval power
- **Submarines**: Hidden strike capability

**Tactical Use**:
- Control strategic chokepoints
- Support land assaults on coastal cities
- Protect trade routes

## Combat Mechanics

### Damage Calculation
- Base damage depends on combat strength difference
- Modifiers from terrain, promotions, and flanking
- Wall defense reduces incoming damage to cities

### Healing
- Units heal faster in friendly territory
- Fortified units gain bonus healing
- Support units can heal adjacent allies

### Experience and Promotions
- Units gain XP from combat
- Promotions provide permanent bonuses
- Choose promotions matching unit role

## Strategic Considerations

### Army Composition
A balanced army for conquest should include:
- 3-4 melee units
- 2-3 ranged units
- 1-2 siege units
- 1-2 cavalry (optional)
- 1 great general (when available)

### Defensive Positioning
- Place ranged units on hills for visibility
- Use terrain chokepoints
- Keep reserve units ready to plug gaps

### Offensive Operations
- Scout enemy positions first
- Attack with numerical superiority
- Focus fire to eliminate units quickly
- Use pillaging to sustain operations

## Unit Upgrades

### Resource Planning
- Track strategic resource availability
- Plan unit compositions around resources
- Consider resource trading for key units

### Timing Considerations
- Upgrade right before major operations
- Save gold for critical upgrades
- Corps/Army formation in late game
