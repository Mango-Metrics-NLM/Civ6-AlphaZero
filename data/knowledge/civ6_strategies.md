# Civilization VI Strategies

## Early Game Fundamentals (Turns 1-50)

### Expansion Priority
- **Settle Early, Settle Often**: The first 3-4 cities should be founded as quickly as possible
- **Scout First**: Always train a scout before your first settler to reveal nearby resources and good city locations
- **Location Factors**: Look for fresh water, luxury resources, and production tiles
- **City Spacing**: Maintain 4-6 tiles between city centers for optimal district placement

### Production Focus
- **Builder Timing**: Get your first builder after securing a second city
- **Infrastructure First**: Focus on granaries and monuments before military units unless threatened
- **District Priority**: Campus or Holy Site should be your first district depending on victory condition

## Military Strategy

### Unit Composition
- **Early Defense**: 2-3 warriors and 1-2 archers can defend most early threats
- **Upgrade Path**: Plan for unit upgrades - warriors become swordsmen, archers become crossbowmen
- **Support Units**: Always include a siege unit when attacking cities

### Combat Tactics
- **Terrain Advantage**: Place melee units on hills and forests for defensive bonuses
- **Flanking**: Position units to attack from multiple sides for bonus damage
- **Zone of Control**: Use ZoC to funnel enemy units into kill zones

## Economic Development

### Gold Management
- **Trade Routes**: Establish internal trade routes early for food/production, switch to gold later
- **Commercial Hubs**: Essential for economic victory conditions
- **Luxury Resources**: Trade excess luxuries for gold or diplomatic favor

### Science Focus
- **Campus Adjacency**: Build campuses next to mountains and rainforests
- **Research Agreements**: Form friendships to enable research agreements
- **Great Scientists**: Prioritize Great Scientist points for tech boosts

## Victory Conditions

### Science Victory
1. Build campuses in every city
2. Rush key techs: Astronomy, Radio, Rocketry, Satellites
3. Complete space race projects in high-production cities

### Domination Victory
1. Build a strong military core by medieval era
2. Target isolated civilizations first
3. Use war weariness breaks strategically

### Cultural Victory
1. Build theater squares and attract great works
2. Use rock bands in late game
3. Open borders and trade routes spread tourism

### Religious Victory
1. Found a religion early with strong beliefs
2. Mass-produce missionaries and apostles
3. Target civilizations with weak faith output

## Diplomatic Considerations

### City-State Relations
- **Suzerain Benefits**: Each city-state type provides unique bonuses
- **Envoy Efficiency**: Focus envoys on 2-3 city-states rather than spreading thin
- **Levy Armies**: Use levied units for early wars without diplomatic penalty

### World Congress
- **Vote Timing**: Save diplomatic favor for crucial votes
- **Resolution Awareness**: Track which resolutions benefit your strategy
