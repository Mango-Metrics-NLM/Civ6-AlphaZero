# Optimal Build Orders

## Standard Opening (Science Focus)

### First City Build Order
1. **Scout** - Explore surroundings and find city locations
2. **Scout** or **Slinger** - Continue exploration or early defense
3. **Settler** - Found second city ASAP
4. **Builder** - Improve tiles in capital
5. **Monument** - Culture for early policies

### Second City Build Order
1. **Monument** - Get culture flowing
2. **Granary** - Population growth
3. **Builder** - Improve resources

### Campus Rush
1. Complete first district (Campus) in capital by turn 30-40
2. Build Library when unlocked
3. Research key early techs: Writing, Astrology, Mining

## Military Opening (Domination Focus)

### Aggressive Build Order
1. **Scout** - Find neighbors
2. **Slinger** - Early defense and promotion
3. **Slinger** - Archery boost requirement
4. **Warrior** - Support for rush
5. **Settler** - Secure territory before war

### Early Rush Composition
- 3 Archers (from Slingers)
- 2 Warriors
- Target: Nearest undefended capital

## Religious Opening (Religion Focus)

### Holy Site Rush
1. **Scout** - Find natural wonders for faith
2. **Monument** - Early culture
3. **Settler** - Claim religious city-states
4. **Holy Site** - Start as soon as possible

### Prophet Race
- Build Holy Site before turn 25
- Use faith purchases for religious units
- Aim for first religion founder bonus

## Cultural Opening (Tourism Focus)

### Theatre Square Priority
1. **Scout** - Locate cultural city-states
2. **Monument** - Essential early culture
3. **Builder** - Plantations for Theatre adjacency
4. **Settler** - Coastal city for future seaside resorts

## Economic Opening (Wide Empire)

### Wide Expansion
1. **Scout** x2 - Maximum exploration
2. **Settler** - Aggressive expansion
3. **Settler** - More cities = more everything
4. **Builder** - Batch improvements

### Commercial Hub Focus
- Place Commercial Hub in each city
- Prioritize trade route capacity
- Internal routes early, international later

## Situational Adjustments

### Threatened by Barbarians
- Add 1-2 Slingers before Settler
- Clear camps for gold and era score

### Close Aggressive Neighbor
- Walls earlier than normal
- Keep standing army of 3-4 units
- Consider preemptive strike

### Isolated Start
- Faster Settler production
- Delay military builds
- Focus on infrastructure

## Tech and Civic Priorities

### Key Early Techs
1. **Pottery** - Granaries
2. **Mining** - Production tiles
3. **Writing** - Campus district

### Key Early Civics
1. **Code of Laws** - Government and policies
2. **Foreign Trade** - Trade routes
3. **Political Philosophy** - First government

## Benchmark Timings

### Competitive Pace
- First Settler: Turn 20-25
- Second Settler: Turn 35-40
- First District: Turn 25-35
- Third City: Turn 45-50

### Recovery Indicators
- Behind on settlers: Buy with gold
- Behind on military: Walls and alliances
- Behind on science: Trade for techs
