# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include showing
empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and
gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the
community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, publishing others' private information without permission, and other
conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainer via a private GitHub Security Advisory on this
repository ("Report a vulnerability" on the Security tab — it is the one private
channel GitHub provides) or a direct message to
[@ianshank](https://github.com/ianshank). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's identity kept confidential.
